TK Nexus ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile application **The System** (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the App.

By using The System, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

### Account Information
- Username (chosen by you)
- Email address (optional, used for account recovery and notifications)
- Password (encrypted; we never see your plain-text password)

### Profile Information
- Profile picture (optional, uploaded by you)
- Hunter rank, level, XP, and achievements
- Equipped items, frames, avatars, and titles
- Guild membership and guild messages

### Usage Data
- Quests completed and daily streaks
- Workout, study, and activity logs you enter
- In-app interactions (follows, leaderboard position)

### Device Information
- Device push notification token (for sending alerts)
- App version and basic crash diagnostics

We do **not** collect your phone number, contacts, location, photos (outside what you upload), or browsing history.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide and maintain the App's features (profile, leaderboard, guilds, quests)
- Send push notifications you've opted into (follow alerts, guild messages, season events)
- Display your public profile to other hunters (username, rank, equipped cosmetics)
- Improve the App's functionality and fix bugs

We do **not** sell your personal data to third parties.
We do **not** show advertisements.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on **Supabase** infrastructure (hosted in the EU region).
We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit.
Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

While we take reasonable precautions, no system is 100% secure. You use the App at your own risk.

## 4. Data Sharing

We share data only in these limited cases:
- **Public profile data** (username, rank, level, equipped cosmetics) is visible to other users via leaderboard and profile screens
- **Guild messages** are visible to members of the same guild
- **Service providers** (Supabase for hosting, Firebase for push notifications) — they process data on our behalf and are bound by their own privacy policies

We do not share your email or private account details with other users.

---
## 5. AI Assistant

The System includes an in-app AI assistant. When a Hunter sends a message (typed or voice) to the assistant:

- The message text, your current hunter stats (level, XP, rank, streak), and the titles of your current quests are sent to our backend (Supabase Edge Functions).
- The backend forwards the message to Groq Inc. (https://groq.com), which runs a large language model to generate a reply.
- For voice messages, the recorded audio is uploaded to our backend and forwarded to Groq's Whisper transcription service. The audio file is not retained after transcription.
- Groq does not retain message content by default and uses it only to process the request. See Groq's privacy policy: https://groq.com/privacy-policy/
- Voice and AI features are completely optional and only activated when initiated by the user.

You can use the app fully without using the AI assistant. The assistant only sends data when you explicitly use it.
